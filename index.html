FROM ENQUIRY TO A CLEARER PROJECT SCOPE

How Your Racking Enquiry Moves Forward

Every project is different. These five steps help turn early information into a scope that can be reviewed, quoted and confirmed.

1

Share Project Inputs

Send load dimensions and weights, quantities, building information, handling equipment, photos and delivery destination.

2

Compare Storage Concepts

Review access, density, stock rotation, aisle use and handling flow before selecting a preferred racking concept.

3

Define Layout & Scope

Record assumptions, usable positions, interfaces, responsibilities, exclusions and the information still requiring confirmation.

4

Standards & Quality Review

Confirm target-market requirements, certificate scope, product drawings, materials, finishes and agreed quality records before production.

5

Production, Delivery & Acceptance Support

Manufacture to the confirmed specification, coordinate delivery and support acceptance against the agreed standards, drawings and quality documents.
