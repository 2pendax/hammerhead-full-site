Pallet Shuttle Racking

Pallet shuttle racking uses a powered carrier to move pallets inside deep storage lanes. Coordinate the carrier, rails, rack and lane-face handling equipment.

Published by zhengdarack · Updated 16 November 2026

The short answer

Pallet shuttle racking combines deep storage lanes with a powered carrier that transports pallets along the lane rails. In a semi-automated installation, a forklift typically places the carrier in the selected lane and handles pallets at the lane face, while an operator commands the carrier. Fully automated arrangements use additional automated handling equipment. Specify the actual automation boundary, pallet envelope, lane allocation and peak handling demand before comparing offers.

How does pallet shuttle racking work?

The forklift and carrier perform different parts of the storage task. The forklift presents or removes pallets at the lane face; the carrier transports them within the designated lane according to the supplied controls.

Mecalux's semi-automated shuttle overview describes an operator-controlled carrier placed in a lane using a forklift. Confirm the supplied method for the actual project.

Which stock profile suits shuttle lanes?

Deep lanes are most useful when quantities can occupy them without excessive unused positions or blocked retrieval. Compare normal and seasonal peaks by SKU and rotation group.

  • Pallets per SKU and batch.
  • Required FIFO or LIFO sequence.
  • Receiving and dispatch batch size.
  • Frequency of lane changes.
  • Exceptional, damaged or quarantined stock.
  • Expected changes in pallet dimensions.

Rotation depends on the layout, access faces and operating rules. Ask the supplier to show how the proposed arrangement meets your sequence. A carrier alone does not guarantee warehouse-wide FIFO.

What pallet data does a shuttle supplier need?

Carrier lifting and sensing must work with the intended pallet and load. Provide samples or underside photographs as well as a dimensioned schedule.

  • Width, depth, height and load overhang.
  • Minimum and maximum loaded weight.
  • Runner direction and underside openings.
  • Load distribution and stability.
  • Permitted pallet wear and rejection criteria.
  • Temperature, moisture and other exposure.

Have non-standard pallets and containers reviewed explicitly. Similar outer dimensions do not prove that the carrier can engage, lift, sense and transport them correctly.

How many shuttle carriers are needed?

Estimate carrier quantity from the complete operating cycle, including lane relocation, pallet handoff, charging and planned service. A carrier's travel speed alone is insufficient.

  1. Map peak loading and retrieval requests.
  2. Assign those requests to lanes and levels.
  3. Include forklift time moving the carrier where applicable.
  4. Include charging or battery-change arrangements.
  5. Review waiting and simultaneous work.
  6. Agree service and downtime provisions.

The stow carrier overview illustrates placement, commanded lane tasks and subsequent relocation. Its specifications are product-specific and should not be treated as universal shuttle performance.

Which layout interfaces should be documented?

Show rack and rail geometry together with carrier clearance and lane-face equipment. Define the position where the forklift presents the pallet and where the carrier receives it.

  • Lane depth, levels and pallet positions.
  • Carrier access, lifting and retrieval interfaces.
  • Truck operating aisle and load envelope.
  • Staging, charging and service locations.
  • Floor, building and protection requirements.
  • Controls, communications and inventory records.

Count usable positions after stock allocation and working areas are reserved. Compare the result with the high-density storage comparison.

What should acceptance and handover include?

Agree representative pallet trials and interface checks before ordering. Record the accepted pallet envelope and operating environment in the final documents.

  1. Verify rack, rails, carriers and identified components.
  2. Check representative loading and retrieval under supplier procedures.
  3. Verify selected control and protective functions.
  4. Record exceptions and corrective work.
  5. Hand over operating instructions and training scope.
  6. Define maintenance, spares and support responsibilities.

Abnormal conditions need a supplied recovery procedure. Access to a loaded lane, disabled carrier or trapped load must be managed by authorised personnel using the assessed method.

Pallet shuttle racking: common questions

Does the forklift enter the storage lane?

In a typical semi-automated shuttle arrangement it handles pallets at the lane face while the carrier moves inside. Confirm the supplied method.

Does one carrier serve every lane?

A carrier may be relocated where the system allows it, but required quantity depends on demand, relocation, charging and availability.

Is shuttle rack automatically FIFO?

No. Rotation depends on access faces, lane allocation and the operating sequence.

Can any pallet be used?

No. Dimensions, underside, weight range and condition must match the approved carrier and rails.

What information improves the first proposal?

Send pallets and loads, SKU quantities, rotation rules, throughput, building plan, equipment, environment and installation location.
