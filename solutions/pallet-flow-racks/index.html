FIFO depends on validated movement

Pallet Flow Rack Planning and Testing Guide

Pallet flow uses gravity lanes so loads enter at a replenishment face and move toward a separate retrieval face. The concept can support first-in/first-out rotation, but lane behavior depends on pallet quality, weight, bottom geometry, pitch, rollers, brakes, stops, temperature, and maintenance.

Short answer: define the approved pallet and load envelope, test the full range on representative lanes, control speed and separation, design replenishment and retrieval, coordinate fire protection, and maintain every lane. This page does not promise automatic FIFO, lanes 20 pallets deep, or nationwide design, engineering, and installation.

Profile pallets and unit loads

Pallet construction

Type, material, stringers or blocks, deck boards, bottom contact, dimensions, tolerances, repairs, protrusions, damage and supplier variation.

Weight range

Minimum, maximum and typical weight; center of gravity; uneven distribution; liquid movement; product shift; partial loads and future variation.

Load geometry

Width, depth, height, overhang, wrap, straps, leaning, compression, stability, labels and interaction with stops or adjacent loads.

Rotation

SKU, lot, date, expiry, receipt sequence, quarantine, returns, recalls, replenishment side, retrieval side and exception handling.

Environment

Ambient, cold, freezer, moisture, ice, dust, debris, corrosion, cleaning, lubricant behavior and temperature during testing and operation.

Throughput

Receipts, puts, retrievals, peaks, lane replenishment, travel, dwell, dock timing, equipment, blocked lanes and urgent access.

Lane components and validation

ComponentFunctionValidation focus
Rollers or wheelsSupport and move the palletContact with pallet underside, spacing, strength, tracking, friction, debris, temperature and service access
PitchCreates gravity forceLightest load restart, heaviest load speed, lane length, pallet condition and site levelness
Speed controllers or brakesLimit load velocity in the specified rangeContact, weight range, spacing, temperature, wear, replacement and failure behavior
Stops and separatorsRetain the front load and may isolate pressure during retrievalImpact, forces, pallet overhang, truck interaction, release timing, inspection and maintenance
Guides and structureMaintain alignment and carry lane loadsPallet tolerance, clearances, entry, frame and beam configuration, anchors, impact and fire interfaces

Test the extremes and realistic defects. The lightest load must advance; the heaviest must remain controlled. Include cold or wet conditions, worn but acceptable pallets, variation from suppliers, restarts after dwell, deepest position, full lane pressure, retrieval and controlled jam response.

Planning and commissioning sequence

Segment the pallet population

Group loads only when pallet underside, dimensions, weight, stability, tracking and rotation are compatible. Assign unsuitable or highly variable loads elsewhere.

Prototype representative lanes

Use proposed components, pitch, depth and environment. Record acceleration, speed, tracking, impact, stopping, restart, separation, retrieval and damaged-pallet behavior.

Design both operating faces

Coordinate replenishment and retrieval equipment, aisles, staging, pedestrian separation, location status, overfill prevention, blocked-lane response and visibility.

Coordinate structure, building and fire

Review total lane loads, rack, anchors, slab, seismic criteria, storage height, commodity, flue spaces, sprinklers, egress, permits and maintenance access.

Commission and control changes

Inspect installation, test agreed cases, train users, establish pallet rejection, cleaning and maintenance, track lane faults, and approve load or packaging changes.

FIFO is an operating system

A gravity lane presents loads in sequence only when replenishment, retrieval, lane assignment, lot status and inventory records remain controlled. Mixed lots, blocked lanes, partial retrieval, returns, quarantine, recalls or manual moves can break the intended rotation. Define how each exception is identified and reconciled.

Do not enter or climb lanes to clear a pallet. Create a safe, product-specific method for isolating the lane, controlling stored energy and loads, accessing components, recovering inventory and authorizing return to service. The method should cover deepest-position faults and failed separators or stops.

Design review questions

Movement

Does every approved load advance, restart, track and stop under the full weight, pallet, environment and dwell range?

Rotation

Can operations preserve lot and date sequence through replenishment, retrieval, exceptions, returns, quarantine and inventory discrepancies?

Equipment

Can the actual trucks place and retrieve loads without striking rollers, guides, stops, separators, rack or adjacent pallets?

Fire protection

Have deep-lane geometry, commodity, pallets, storage height, flue spaces, sprinklers, access and permits been reviewed?

Maintenance

Can rollers, brakes, stops, separators, guides and structure be inspected, cleaned and replaced without unsafe lane entry?

Change control

Who approves new pallets, loads, packaging, lane assignments, pitch, components, environment, equipment and operating logic?

Information for a pallet flow brief

Provide pallet samples and specifications, minimum and maximum loads, product geometry, SKU and lot quantities, rotation, throughput, environment, equipment, building survey, slab and fire documents, target lane depth, current issues, growth, schedule, and responsible reviewers.

Require proposals to state the approved pallet envelope, test plan, pitch, rollers, brakes, stops, separators, guides, load basis, lane depth, fire assumptions, maintenance, spare parts, installation, inspection, training, permits, acceptance criteria and exclusions.
