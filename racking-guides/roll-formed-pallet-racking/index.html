Roll Formed Pallet Racking

Roll formed pallet racking uses steel sheet or coil shaped at room temperature into efficient rack members. Profile, connection and configuration determine performance.

Published by zhengdarack · Updated 16 November 2026

Plan Your Rack Specification →
The short answer

Roll formed pallet racking uses cold-formed steel profiles for upright columns, beams or both. Steel sheet or coil passes through forming operations that create folds and ribs, allowing efficient member shapes. Many systems use boltless beam connectors and repeated upright openings for adjustment. The category has no universal capacity or compatibility: compare exact profiles, connections, loads, configuration and site requirements.

How is roll formed pallet racking made?

Roll forming shapes a strip of steel through a sequence of forming stations at room temperature. The bends, returns and ribs create a rack profile from relatively thin material without implying a simple flat-sheet behaviour.

The Rack Manufacturers Institute glossary defines a cold-formed steel structural member as a shape made by press braking or roll forming sheet, coil or plate without the heat used for hot forming.

Why do roll formed column profiles look different?

Manufacturers use different open, partially closed or closed profiles, bends, ribs, hole patterns and bracing details. These features belong to a tested and analysed component system; visual similarity does not establish equivalent performance.

Do not rank rack proposals by sheet thickness alone. Section geometry, material, bracing, connection behaviour, beam spacing and the complete configuration all matter.

Are roll formed rack beams always boltless?

Boltless beam connections are common, but they are not guaranteed by the material-forming method. Confirm the supplied beam-end connector, upright punching and locking device.

  1. Identify the manufacturer and product series.
  2. Match beam and upright component references.
  3. Check that every connector is completely seated.
  4. Confirm the specified lock is present and engaged.
  5. Install and inspect using the supplied instructions.

Steel King's SK2000 assembly guide is one manufacturer-specific example: it shows its beam rivets entering teardrop openings and a spring clip engaging. That detail should not be assumed for another system.

For connection identification rather than manufacturing method, use the teardrop pallet rack guide.

When should roll formed pallet racking be considered?

Roll formed rack can be evaluated for selective pallet storage where adjustable levels, efficient components and a boltless assembly option suit the operation. The decision must come from comparable project designs.

  • Many SKUs need direct pallet access.
  • Pallet heights may change within approved configurations.
  • The buyer values readily identified replacement components.
  • Loads, beam spans and frame heights fit an engineered roll formed solution.
  • The planned protection and inspection program addresses handling exposure.

RMI's standards catalogue includes storage racks made from cold-formed and hot-rolled members within its stated scope. Material category alone does not waive design, installation or use requirements.

Compare the wider material-choice question in the structural pallet racking selection guide.

Which information establishes a roll formed rack proposal?

Request a design tied to the real unit loads and final configuration. A component capacity table is not a complete rack proposal.

  • Unit loads: maximum pallet dimensions, weight, height and underside.
  • Level: beam span, number and placement of pallets and supports.
  • Frame: height, depth, beam elevations, bracing and base details.
  • Layout: rows, bays, aisles, working areas and usable positions.
  • Equipment: forklift, attachment, lift height and clearances.
  • Site: floor information, environment, location and project requirements.
Do not convert appearance into capacity

Two columns with a similar outline can use different steel, geometry, punching, bracing and connections. Use the identified product's project documentation.

Can roll formed pallet rack be reconfigured freely?

Repeated holes make beam movement possible, but not every spacing and loading arrangement is approved. Moving or adding a level changes the frame configuration and the total assigned load.

  1. Record the existing product, beam elevations and loads.
  2. Define the proposed pallets, levels and accessories.
  3. Obtain the required review against product and project documentation.
  4. Implement with identified components and instructions.
  5. Update drawings, load signs and operating records.

Do not straighten, weld, heat or drill a damaged roll formed member as an improvised repair. Isolate uncertain rack condition under site procedures and obtain an appropriate assessment. See the adjustable pallet racking guide for a change-control workflow.

Roll formed pallet racking: common questions

Is roll formed pallet rack made with hot steel?

The source coil may have its own production history, but roll forming the rack profile is a cold-forming operation performed without deliberate heating for shaping.

Is roll formed rack always weaker than structural rack?

No universal comparison is valid. Compare engineered systems for the same loads, geometry, connections, site and applicable requirements.

Are all roll formed beams interchangeable?

No. Identify the upright, beam connector, lock and manufacturer documentation before combining components.

Can beam levels be moved without bolts?

Physical movement may be quick, but the revised spacing and loads still require the project's change-control review and updated records.

What information improves a first quotation?

Provide pallet dimensions and weights, desired positions, beam levels, a floor plan, forklift details, environment, site location and required project scope.
