Longspan Shelving

Longspan shelving creates wider hand-loaded storage openings for cartons, bins and bulky items. Specify the usable opening and complete level construction together.

Published by zhengdarack · Updated 16 November 2026

Plan Your Rack Specification →
The short answer

Longspan shelving is a hand-loaded shelving system with wider shelf openings than many small-parts units. Choose it by the largest stored item, clear opening, shelf depth, contact pattern and maximum load per level and bay. The outside bay width is not the same as usable shelf width.

When does longspan shelving fit the storage task?

Consider longspan shelving when staff handle cartons, bins or individual bulky goods that need a wider opening than small-parts shelving. If goods remain on forklift-loaded pallets, begin with pallet racking. If they are long bundles, compare cantilever storage.

Interlake Mecalux describes wide-span shelving as hand-loaded storage for medium to heavy loads. Use the manufacturer's description to understand the category, then request project-specific documentation for the system offered.

How do you specify a long shelf opening?

Use clear dimensions between obstructions, not only overall rack dimensions. Posts, beam connectors and shelf edges consume space. Ask for usable width, usable height and supported depth on the proposal drawing.

For example, two cartons 480 mm wide occupy 960 mm before allowing for a gap, dividers or hand access. That does not prove they fit a nominal 1,000 mm bay. Request the usable opening and trial representative cartons.

Which parts make up a longspan shelving level?

A level normally involves beams, connections, a shelf surface and any intermediate supports. Treat those parts as one rated arrangement. Replacing the surface or changing the beam span can change the loading condition.

  • Beam span: clear distance and connection used in the proposed bay.
  • Shelf surface: steel panel, board, wire or another specified material.
  • Intermediate supports: quantity, position and compatibility with the surface.
  • Frames and bracing: complete bay arrangement and overall loading.

Send narrow feet, runners or uneven bases for review. A distributed-load value cannot automatically be assigned to equipment that rests on a few small contact points. For more on this distinction, see industrial steel shelving load terminology.

How should longspan shelves support picking and replenishment?

Describe where the picker stands, how the item is gripped and where replenishment stock waits. Shelf depth and level height affect whether rear or upper goods remain practical to handle.

  1. List the heaviest, largest and most frequently handled goods.
  2. Place representative containers in a trial opening.
  3. Check label visibility, grip space and withdrawal path.
  4. Test replenishment while another normal task occurs nearby.
  5. Record any handling aid required for the final layout.

A shelf's structural rating does not establish that the lifting or reaching task is suitable. Review manual handling separately with the people and equipment involved.

Can longspan shelf levels be repositioned?

Many products are adjustable, but physical adjustability does not prove that every layout has the same capacity. Moving levels changes spacing and the loading carried by the complete bay.

Keep drawings, load information and component identification. Before adding or moving a level, check the approved configurations with the manufacturer or responsible qualified reviewer. Update load information when the permitted arrangement changes.

Do not mix similar-looking beams, connectors or shelf panels without documented compatibility. Colour and physical fit are not enough evidence.

Longspan shelving: common questions

Is longspan shelving the same as pallet racking?

No assumption should be made from appearance. Longspan shelving is commonly hand loaded; forklift-loaded pallets require a system documented for that operation.

Does a longer shelf opening hold more weight?

Not automatically. Request the rated configuration for the exact span, beams, surface, supports and load distribution.

Can board decking be replaced with steel panels?

Have the proposed panel and supports reviewed as part of the complete level. A different surface may change load transfer and environmental suitability.

How deep should longspan shelving be?

Choose depth around the item, access from one or both sides and the retrieval task. Deeper shelving can make rear goods harder to reach.

What information is needed for a quote?

Send item dimensions and weights, contact points, quantity per level, clear-space needs, handling method, site dimensions and required services.

Prepare a longspan shelving specification

Send the biggest item and the busiest picking task

Include maximum dimensions, weight, contact points, quantity per level, shelf opening needs, available space and delivery location.
