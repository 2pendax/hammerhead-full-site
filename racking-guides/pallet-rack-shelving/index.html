Pallet Rack Shelving

Pallet rack shelving adds a support surface or accessory to beam levels. Specify the stored item and its contact points before choosing wire deck, crossbars or another surface.

Published by zhengdarack · Updated 16 November 2026

Plan Your Rack Specification →
The short answer

Pallet rack shelving combines a pallet-rack frame and beam structure with supports or decking selected for the stored load. It may support pallets, cartons, bins or hand-picked goods, but those uses are not interchangeable. State whether the load rests on pallet runners, small feet or a continuous base, and obtain ratings for the complete proposed level.

When does a pallet rack level need shelving or support accessories?

Start with the item that will contact the level. A conventional pallet may bridge the rack beams in a defined orientation, while a carton, tote or irregular skid may need a continuous or additional support arrangement. Do not select an accessory from its appearance alone.

UNARCO's pallet-rack component overview identifies crossbars and wire decking as different support accessories. Its product information applies to its own equipment; use it to understand terminology, not to rate another system.

How do wire deck, crossbars and solid surfaces differ?

The support option must be evaluated with the load and beam configuration. A wire surface, crossbar or board cannot be assigned a universal capacity independent of its dimensions, supports and installation.

  • Crossbars: discuss where they sit, which pallet runners or load points they support and how many the proposed level uses.
  • Wire decking: provide the item footprint and confirm mesh, support channels, beam fit and the documented loading condition.
  • Solid surfaces: specify the panel, supports, environment and fire-protection implications with the responsible project parties.
  • Shelf beams or picking levels: describe the manual-picking load separately from pallet storage above or beside it.

Open and solid surfaces can affect visibility, cleaning and the way small items are retained. These practical features do not replace structural documentation. Ask for the accessory model, size and approved use to appear in the quotation and final component list.

For manufacturer examples of beams intended for picking levels, see the stow conventional pallet-rack component page. Do not transfer its dimensions or capacities to a different product.

Why should the load underside be part of the enquiry?

A top photograph shows the goods; an underside photograph shows how the load reaches the rack. Include runners, blocks, feet, damaged boards and any stretch wrap or packaging that projects below the intended support plane.

Also state the placement tolerance. A load positioned slightly left or right may contact supports differently. If damaged or nonstandard pallets occur in the operation, do not hide that condition from the design discussion.

How should a pallet rack picking level be specified?

Describe whether workers pick full cartons, individual items or complete pallets. Show the replenishment side and the picking side, along with the equipment operating nearby. A shelf surface can make an opening usable for cartons, but it does not resolve reach, lifting or traffic questions.

List the maximum load on the picking level and the intended distribution across it. Separate this from the pallet loads on other levels. For the wider picking process, use the warehouse shelving systems guide; for pallet-level terminology, use the pallet racking basics page.

Trial representative cartons and bins before repeating the layout. Check whether labels remain visible, whether the rear items can be reached and where replenishment containers wait. Record any change to shelf height or surface in the final proposal.

What should a pallet rack shelving quotation identify?

  1. Rack configuration: upright frames, beam spans, level positions and documented loads.
  2. Accessory: type, dimensions, quantity, supports and exact level location.
  3. Stored load: pallet or container dimensions, underside construction, weight and placement.
  4. Installation: who installs each accessory and which instructions apply.
  5. Handover: drawings, component identification, load information and change-control process.

UNARCO's installation instructions place shelf accessories such as crossbars or wire decking within the rack installation sequence. Follow the instructions for the actual manufacturer and project rather than treating general online guidance as approval.

If accessories are being added to an existing rack, provide manufacturer identification and the current configuration. Similar-looking beams or decks are not proof of compatibility. Ask for the proposed addition to be documented with the complete rack arrangement.

Pallet rack shelving: common questions

Does wire decking increase the beam capacity?

Do not assume so. Request ratings for the complete level, including the beams, deck, supports and actual load condition. An accessory cannot be treated as a universal capacity upgrade.

Can cartons be stored directly on pallet rack beams?

Specify a suitable support surface for the cartons and obtain documentation for the intended loading. Beam spacing alone does not establish that a carton can be stored safely.

Are crossbars and wire decks the same thing?

No. They are different accessories with different construction and support roles. The proposal should identify exactly which item is supplied and where it is installed.

Can wood boards be added to an existing rack?

Have the material, supports, loading, environment and applicable fire requirements reviewed before making the change. Do not add an unidentified surface based only on physical fit.

What photos should I send?

Send the full load, underside contact points, pallet or container identification, the existing rack connections and wide views of the intended storage area.
