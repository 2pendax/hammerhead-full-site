Bulk Storage Racks

Bulk storage racks should match the size, weight and handling method of oversized goods. Define what bulk means in your operation before choosing a rack family.

Published by zhengdarack · Updated 16 November 2026

Plan Your Rack Specification →
The short answer

Bulk storage racks hold large cartons, containers or individual units that need more shelf opening than small-parts shelving. Hand-loaded bulky goods may suit wide-span shelving, palletized goods may require pallet racking, and long items may require cantilever storage. Specify the actual load before treating bulk rack as one product type.

What does bulk storage rack mean for your goods?

Bulk can describe physical size, quantity or both. A lightweight empty carton can occupy a large opening, while a compact machine can create a demanding point load. Record dimensions and weight separately.

MHI's rack fundamentals page describes cartons, pallet loads and large individual units as rack-stored items. Interlake Mecalux describes wide-span shelving as hand-loaded storage. These references establish general categories, not a rating for a proposed rack.

Which dimensions matter for oversized goods?

Measure the load as it will be stored, not only the product inside its packaging. Include handles, projections, wrapping, skids and any container that remains on the shelf.

Record the usable opening between obstructions, not just the rack's outside width. Check shelf depth, rear projection and the room needed to grip, turn or lift the item. For a 1,100 mm carton, a nominal 1,100 mm bay is not proof of fit.

How should bulky goods be loaded and retrieved?

Describe whether people, a trolley, lift table or forklift moves the load. State the approach direction and the space needed to transfer it. A hand-loaded shelf and a forklift-loaded pallet position are different applications even if the goods have the same weight.

  • Manual handling: assess the whole task, including reach, grip and awkward shape.
  • Mechanical aid: show where the equipment approaches and how the load transfers.
  • Forklift handling: provide the pallet or skid underside and intended truck details.
  • Mixed access: separate worker picking from vehicle routes and replenishment.

Do not infer that a shelf capacity makes a lifting task acceptable. Include the people and equipment responsible for the movement when reviewing the layout.

What capacity and support information should be documented?

Provide the maximum individual load, total load per level and complete bay loading. Show how each item contacts the shelf: spread base, runners, feet or edges. Ask for documentation that matches that load pattern.

Wide openings, shelf surfaces and supports work together. A panel, wire deck or board cannot be treated as an independent capacity upgrade. Identify every component and its location on the final drawing.

Planning arithmetic only

Three containers at 180 kg each represent 540 kg of stored mass on a level. This addition does not select the beams, surface, supports or rack. The supplier still needs dimensions, contact points and placement.

For point-load and surface questions, use the heavy duty storage rack guide. For a hand-loaded steel bay, see industrial steel shelving.

What belongs in a bulk storage rack quotation?

  1. Load schedule: item dimensions, weights, contact points and quantities.
  2. Rack drawing: overall size, clear openings, shelf levels and usable depth.
  3. Rated configuration: shelf or level and complete bay limits for the proposed arrangement.
  4. Handling plan: manual or mechanical process and required access.
  5. Project scope: finish, accessories, freight, installation and handover documents.

Ask suppliers to list assumptions and exclusions. Compare the same number of usable locations and the same service scope rather than comparing only rack length or the price of steel components.

Bulk storage racks: common questions

Are bulk storage racks the same as pallet racks?

Not always. Bulk rack is a broad buying term. Identify whether goods are hand loaded, palletized, long or irregular before choosing a system.

Is a wider shelf always better?

No. Choose the opening around the stored load and required handling space, then verify the rated configuration. Excess width may waste space or change support needs.

Can bulky cartons be stored on wire decking?

Provide carton dimensions, base condition and weight for review. The mesh, supports, beam fit and load distribution must suit the actual carton.

Can workers climb the rack to reach large items?

No access method should be assumed from the rack itself. Plan suitable retrieval equipment and procedures for the intended level and task.

What photos help a quotation?

Send the complete load from several sides, its underside, the handling process and the intended storage area with dimensions.
