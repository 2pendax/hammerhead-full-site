What a supplier still needs to verify

Loads and configuration

Exact unit loads, rack components, elevations, supports, anchors, slab, seismic criteria, handling and operating rules determine the design. A text brief is only a starting point.

Building and fire protection

Measured conditions, storage height, commodity, sprinklers, flue spaces, egress, permits and local requirements need review by responsible professionals and authorities.

Commercial scope

Confirm exclusions, freight, taxes, site access, unloading, inventory moves, permits, other trades, schedule, inspections, warranty, payment and change procedures.

Privacy: this tool runs locally in your current browser page. The Generate, Copy, and Download buttons do not send the entered information to zhengdarack or a third party. If you choose to share the brief, review it first and remove confidential, personal, security-sensitive, export-controlled, or regulated information.

Before requesting a firm quotation

A firm price should reference an agreed scope and revision. Attach dimensioned building information, load data, photographs that do not expose sensitive information, material-handling equipment specifications, and any available fire-protection or existing-rack documents. State what is unknown rather than filling gaps with guesses.

Ask each bidder to identify assumptions and exclusions. Compare the same responsibilities: engineering, permit support, freight, unloading, installation, anchors, protection, floor repair, sprinkler work, inspection, labeling, cleanup, training, and disposal. A low number may simply omit a task that another quotation includes.

Use the contact page if you want to ask whether a specific scope can be reviewed. Sending an inquiry does not by itself create a contract, reserve inventory, establish a schedule, or confirm service availability.
