Why the old address has a current notice

The page address may already appear in search results, analytics, bookmarks, automated summaries, or third-party references. Returning only an error could leave cached claims unexplained. A notice at the same address lets a reader see that the former wording is no longer endorsed.

This page is not an event listing or a press release. It does not assert that the company attended MODEX 2026, reserved a booth, exhibited a product, or was included in an official program. The current page uses ordinary webpage metadata rather than event or news structured data.

The path contains a former brand identifier solely to preserve continuity with the old URL. It is not evidence of a current exhibitor name or legal entity.

Exhibitor status

A company blog post alone does not establish registration, booth assignment, sponsorship, or inclusion in an official event directory.

The legacy wording does not prove that a calculator or any other product was launched, demonstrated, endorsed, or available through the event.

Meetings and representatives

Do not assume named personnel, scheduled appointments, speaking roles, travel, or on-site support without current direct confirmation.

Affiliation or endorsement

Mentioning an event name does not imply that its organizer approves, sponsors, certifies, or is affiliated with this website or its tools.

Practical guidance: if you plan travel, a meeting, a purchase, or media coverage based on event participation, confirm the exhibitor and booth through the organizer’s current official sources and then confirm the specific representative directly.

How to verify a trade-show announcement

Check the organizer’s official directory

Use the official event website or app rather than a copied list. Match the exact exhibitor name and review the directory’s update date.

Confirm the booth and hall

A booth number should map to the same organization on the current floor plan. Booth assignments can change, so recheck close to the visit.

Confirm the representative

Contact the organization through a known channel. Verify dates, hours, meeting location, and the identity of the person expected to attend.

Separate attendance from participation

A visitor badge, private meeting, exhibitor booth, conference session, sponsorship, and product demonstration are different activities. State only the activity supported by evidence.

Record changes

If plans are cancelled or altered, update the announcement, structured data, calendars, and contact instructions promptly so old details do not mislead visitors.

About the warehouse capacity calculator

The website currently provides a separate educational warehouse capacity calculator. Its existence on this site does not prove that it was exhibited, debuted, endorsed, or presented at MODEX 2026.

Calculator results are planning estimates based on user inputs and simplified geometry. They are not an engineered rack design, a code review, a fire-protection analysis, or a promise of usable capacity. Columns, walls, doors, egress, lift equipment, load characteristics, clearances, sprinklers, staging, and local requirements can materially reduce or reshape a concept.

Before using an estimate for purchasing or construction, verify dimensions and loads and obtain review from the qualified professionals and authorities responsible for the actual facility.
