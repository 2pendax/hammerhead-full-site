INTERACTIVE WAREHOUSE SELF-AUDIT

Facility Optimization Checklist

Review 16 practical controls across building data, material flow, storage, traffic, inventory, maintenance and growth.

Original checklist by zhengdarack · Updated 22 November 2026

Scope: this checklist supports a management review. It does not replace a competent structural, rack, forklift, fire-protection or regulatory inspection.
0 of 16 reviewed

1. Building and data

Survey columns, doors, docks, clear heights, overhead services, floor joints and restricted areas; mark unknowns.

Use maximum credible load dimensions and gross weights plus SKU-level inventory distributions, not only averages.

2. Receiving and storage

Separate accepted, held, damaged and unidentified receipts; prevent staging from blocking travel or fire access.

Match selective, deep-lane, dynamic, shelving or automated storage to each product family's access and handling needs.

Verify field configuration against approved documents; control beam moves, repairs and component substitutions.

3. Movement and access

Use the exact truck, mast, attachment and load data; include guards, overhang, turns and local pinch points.

Map crossings and blind corners; define right-of-way, speed, parking and pedestrian protection.

Walk every route with doors open and normal staging present; preserve required working and emergency access.

Coordinate actual commodity, storage height, shelving and rack geometry with the fire-protection design.

4. Inventory and operations

Use order and movement data; account for replenishment and ergonomics instead of slotting by intuition.

Scan or confirm source, item, quantity and destination; provide a controlled exception path.

Stop loading affected areas, keep people away and obtain competent assessment and approved correction.

Provide safe charging, preventive maintenance, fault isolation, critical spares and recovery procedures.

5. Control and growth

Use a small balanced dashboard with owner and action; do not improve one measure by hiding work in another queue.

Require review and revision control for rack levels, loads, aisles, fire conditions, equipment and building work.

Model inventory, SKU and throughput scenarios; reserve expansion paths and define action before capacity is exhausted.

How to use the checklist

  1. Walk the facility with operations, safety, maintenance and inventory representatives.
  2. For each item, record the evidence rather than checking from memory.
  3. Control any immediate hazard before continuing normal work.
  4. Create an action with owner, due date and specialist input where needed.
  5. Recheck completed work and retain the evidence.
  6. Repeat after major changes or at a risk-based regular interval.

Facility checklist questions

Is this a safety inspection?

No. It is a management self-audit and does not replace competent specialist inspections.

How often should it be used?

Use it after major changes and at an interval suited to traffic, growth and risk.

What happens after an unchecked item?

Record evidence, owner, due date and specialist review. Control immediate hazards first.

Does a high score prove compliance?

No. The score is only a progress aid; compliance needs project- and jurisdiction-specific evidence.
