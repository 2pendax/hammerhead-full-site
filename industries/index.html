What changes between industries?

Load interface

Pallet, tote, box, bin, drum, long product and irregular equipment need different support, clearances and handling.

Inventory rule

Direct access, FIFO, FEFO, lot, batch, security, quarantine and client ownership determine usable capacity.

Environment

Cold, humidity, dust, washdown, corrosion, cleanliness and controlled temperature affect materials and maintenance.

Flow

Docks, replenishment, picking, production supply, returns and carrier cut-offs create different bottlenecks.

Risk and regulation

Commodity, fire protection, hazardous materials, food safety, healthcare controls and public access require specific review.

Change pattern

Seasonality, client turnover, promotions, product launches and manufacturing changeovers affect flexibility.

A common planning method

  1. Verify the building: columns, clear heights, slab, docks, exits, sprinklers and utilities.
  2. Classify loads: maximum weight, dimensions, support, stability and environmental needs.
  3. Profile inventory: SKU or item quantities, peaks, rotation, batch and status rules.
  4. Map flow: receiving, storage, replenishment, picking or production, returns and shipping.
  5. Select alternatives: compare selective, deep-lane, dynamic, shelving and automated zones.
  6. Calculate usable capacity: account for compatibility, honeycombing, buffers and blocked positions.
  7. Coordinate design: structure, forklifts, people, fire protection, utilities and permits.
  8. Validate peaks and failures: test congestion, maintenance, exceptions and growth.

This method keeps storage equipment connected to the operation. A high-density concept is not valuable if it hides stock, blocks rotation or cannot meet peak demand.

Inputs for a useful first layout

  • Current building plan or survey.
  • Maximum load dimensions, weight and support footprint.
  • Inventory by item or SKU over time.
  • Inbound, outbound and internal movements by peak period.
  • Rotation, batch, expiry, status and security rules.
  • Forklift or handling-equipment data.
  • Commodity and fire-protection information.
  • Environment, sanitation and maintenance requirements.
  • Growth, schedule, destination and project responsibility.
